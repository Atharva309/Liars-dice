    Take a seat

    1. Everyone hides their dice under a cup. You only see your own.
    2. Claim or challenge. Turns go clockwise. On your turn, claim how many dice of one number are on the whole table, counting every cup, like "at least 3 fives". Each claim must beat the last one. Or call Liar! on the claim before you.
    3. Cups up. If the claim was true, the caller loses a die. If it was false, the claimer does. Last player with dice wins.

    How to play

    1. Hidden dice. Each player rolls their dice under a cup: 2, 3 or 5 each, your choice. You see only yours, on the rack in front of you.
    2. A claim is about every die on the table. "At least 3 fives" means: counting all cups together, three or more dice show a five. You don't need to hold them yourself, which is why you can claim more dice than you own.
    3. Raise it. A new claim must be higher: more dice ("3 fives" to "4 twos"), or the same count with a higher number ("3 fives" to "3 sixes").
    4. Or call Liar! on the claim just before yours. All cups lift and the dice are counted.
    5. Someone loses a die, and nobody ever gains one. Claim true: the caller loses one. Claim false: the claimer loses one. No dice left and you're out. Last player in wins.
    6. Rule of thumb. Each hidden die shows any given number 1 time in 6. Divide the hidden dice by 6 to see how many of a number to expect out there, then add your own.
